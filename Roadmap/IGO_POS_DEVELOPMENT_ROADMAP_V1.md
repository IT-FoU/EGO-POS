 # EGO POS Development Roadmap v1.0

Company: IGO Technology
Product: EGO POS

Mission:
Build a modern POS ecosystem for Go BOX first,
then expand into a SaaS platform for retail businesses across Laos.

Slogan:
Simple. Smart. Fast.

---

# DEVELOPMENT STRATEGY

Do NOT build everything at once.

Build phase by phase.

Each phase must be:

1. Designed
2. Developed
3. Tested
4. Fixed
5. Approved

before moving to next phase.

---
